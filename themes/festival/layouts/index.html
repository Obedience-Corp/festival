Camp Hardening: progress replay

A historical replay of the Camp Hardening festival's recorded progress. Festival turns a long-running goal into structured work, with a record you can return to and review.

How it works

Create a camp, hand off a goal, and review the work as it progresses.

01Set up

Create a camp for your work

A camp holds the projects, plans, and context for one part of your life: a job, a side project, or a hobby. Create the camp, add your repositories, and give your agent a place to keep the work.

{{ with site.GetPage "/getting-started/quickstart" }} Create your camp → {{ end }}
camp init
02Delegate

Describe the work to your agent

Open your agent of choice, such as Claude Code, Codex, or Grok build. Describe the outcome and ask it to prepare a Festival plan. Review the scope and checkpoints before execution. Your agent handles the fest commands.

{{ with site.GetPage "/getting-started/quickstart" }} Try your first handoff → {{ end }}
grok
03Watch

Watch the plan get built

Your agent follows the fest next loop while you focus on other work. Check progress when you need to, review the results, and continue from the saved work record in your next session.

{{ with site.GetPage "/getting-started/quickstart" }} Run and review the work → {{ end }}
fest watch
04Track

Track everything in one place

Every festival, intent, design, and project across the campaign in one dashboard. Run many in parallel with different agents, and switch between them without losing the thread.

An operating environment for the life of the work

Goals evolve over months and years. Keep the plans, decisions, research, and progress with the work itself, so agents and people can return to it, understand it, and continue.

A goal becomes a plan
Phases, sequences, and tasks turn a goal into a graph of work, with dependencies and checks attached.
Context stays with the camp
Related repositories, research, and decisions share a home. Separate camps keep different parts of your life distinct.
Sessions can pick up the work
Saved plans and progress give the next agent a starting point. fest next provides the next step and its planning context.
You have room for other work
Let agents execute approved work in separate festivals and worktrees. Return for decisions, review, and the results.
